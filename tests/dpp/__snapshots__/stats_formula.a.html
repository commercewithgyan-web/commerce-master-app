A1ORIGINAL PROBLEM RECAP4 Marks
State the formula for Karl Pearson's coefficient of correlation using actual mean method and calculate correlation between X and Y where Σxy = 120, Σx² = 160, and Σy² = 250.
Karl Pearson's Coefficient of Correlation (r)
r =Σ xy√Σ x² · Σ y²
STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATION
1. Given data:
• Sum of products of deviations: Σxy = 120
• Sum of squared deviations of X: Σx² = 160
• Sum of squared deviations of Y: Σy² = 250
2. Product of squared deviations:
Σx² · Σy² = 160 · 250 = 40,000
3. Denominator:
√40,000 = 200
4. Final Coefficient:
r = 120 / 200 = +0.60
Interpretation: There is a moderate positive correlation (r = +0.60) between variable X and variable Y.
