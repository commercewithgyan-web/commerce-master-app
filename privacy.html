Privacy Policy

Commerce Gyan Uploader is a private tool used only by Commerce Gyan (Gyan Sir) to upload class recordings to its own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

The app asks for permission to upload videos to the YouTube channel of the account owner (the youtube.upload scope). It cannot read, edit or delete other videos.

Data we store

An access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data of students or visitors, and no data is sent to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
