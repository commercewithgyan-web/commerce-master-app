[Q1] CBSE 2024 (Set 67/5/1) — [3 Marks]
Sunny and Rohan were partners in a firm sharing profits and losses in the ratio of 2 : 1. The capital employed by the firm was ₹7,00,000. The average profits of the firm were ₹90,000 and the normal rate of return was 10%.
Calculate the value of goodwill of the firm on the basis of 5 years' purchase of super profits.


✍️ STEP-BY-STEP WORKING NOTES & SOLUTION:


Normal Profit = Capital Employed × Normal Rate of Return
              = ₹ 7,00,000 × 10% = ₹ ________________________


Super Profit  = Average Profit − Normal Profit
              = ₹ 90,000 − ₹ _______________ = ₹ ________________________


Goodwill      = Super Profit × Number of Years' Purchase
              = ₹ _______________ × 5 Years = ₹ ________________________
